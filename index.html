The Way of Amovera

I vetted Creating Ethics against the standard I now hold, and it did not hold up well to scrutiny. So I have taken it off the site. It is no longer open to the public, and it is not coming back in that form. I would rather say that plainly than leave a work standing on foundations I no longer trust. I took the first entry of this vlog down for the same reason — it was promoting a work that will no longer be public.

What replaces it is The Way of Amovera: an outline, in progress, of the whole system, and the full work will be written by hand out of it. Every item in that outline is tagged — Evidence where research supports it, Preference where it is a value choice I am making and cannot test, Design where it is an untested proposal to pilot. Every source is named at the foot of the page, so the claims can be checked and I can be shown wrong.

That is the standard I am holding to in my philosophical work from here, and it is the standard these vlogs will be held to as well.

What S.T.A.M.P. is

Sovereign Thought Against Manipulated Politics is a framework I have been building to fight back against political manipulation of populations, and the interference of corporations and nations on the public mind — a mind that ought to be clear enough to make rational decisions of a political nature.

The theory and the practice are set out in The Way of Amovera, which is where the written argument now lives. Read on its own it is dense, so these vlogs are meant as the better entry point into the material itself.

Who I am

I have had many names, but for now call me Jack. I am no one of importance, as far as anyone is concerned. I have no special skills other than an obsession to learn and a compulsion to make the world a better place than I left it.

My work is one of systems design and engineering, and though I have tried to do things in order — trying to help the poor, or earn money to build, or to organize people — I found all things have failed to breach the apathy of modern consumerist culture. So I am doing things backwards here, starting with investigative journalism and discussing philosophy on vlogs.

I came to Victoria from Northern BC, and I intend to make my time here count towards the maturation of my fellow people to whatever degree I can.

What Amovera is

Amovera is the recognition that every paradigm is a tool used to direct human behavior, and that like every other tool it is made by humanity — crafted and built in our minds as reflections of the world we see around us.

Nothing is set in stone, but temperance is needed to make anything work. Amovera seeks to bring that balance into focus in the minds of those around us, so that the minds themselves can come up with the next generation of solutions to our social, economic and political problems.
