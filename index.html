What S.T.A.M.P. is

Sovereign Thought Against Manipulated Politics. It is the investigative and recruitment instrument of the Amovera system, carried in pamphlets and zines — and now in these films.

Its subject is the machinery by which political thought is manufactured, and what a person can do about it from where they already stand.

Who I am

I am Jack M. N. Gladstone — an independent philosopher and writer in northern British Columbia, author of Creating Ethics and originator of the Amovera system. I am self-educated, and I have kept myself through entry-level work across every level of society. That breadth is not incidental to the philosophy: it is meant to be carried by working people rather than deposited in a discipline.

The work is signed Jack of Suits, and the figure is fourfold. The Jack of Diamonds writes the philosophy. The Jack of Clubs — whose office this is — writes the investigative work and the pamphlets. The Jack of Spades and the Jack of Hearts hold their own offices, to be disclosed in time. All four names and the legal name are public; the private person behind them is kept deliberately separate.

What Amovera is

A philosophical system built over roughly fifteen years of dialectical work — by question and counter-question, derivation rather than assertion. Its Master Principle was arrived at rather than posited:

All that persists does so by acquiring what it lacks.

Eight frameworks hang from that spine, each the same principle worked out in a different domain of human life. S.T.A.M.P. is one of the eight.

It is not a party and not a programme, and it belongs to no one. The aim is not personal: it is to lay down something another person can carry forward.

A note on what used to be here. Until October 2026 this address carried the Archiome Commons — a field manual of fifty-nine technics with a governance system for admitting more. That work is preserved and is not lost; it is simply no longer the thing this site is for.
